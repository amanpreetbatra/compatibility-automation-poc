How This Pipeline Works

1. Stage docsSample vendor documents are copied into docs/raw/.
2. Detect changesSHA-256 hashing identifies document deltas and records a pending queue.
3. Extract textPlain text is preserved as-is and non-text formats are stubbed with provenance.
4. AI analysisGitHub Models optionally extracts envelope evidence and risk statements into JSON.
5. Extract envelopeAI output is validated against the kernel regex, then regex fallback runs if needed.
6. Diff rulesThe generated rule is compared with the baseline to classify change safety.
7. Generate testsGO and NO_GO kernel cases are emitted for the compatibility engine.
8. DashboardA dark-theme audit view summarizes verdict, evidence, metadata, and tests.
